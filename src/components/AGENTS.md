# Traffic component rules

- The Private Pilot oblique view always flies the full runway-13 left-hand VFR pattern, because only it lies in front of the fixed camera; live weather shapes climb, gust jitter and night dimming, never closes the pattern. Skip projected depths <150m and break guide paths across gaps to prevent behind-camera explosions.
- Journeys are scroll-driven (tail 0.5 stage after the last stage so a fling still shows it) (src/lib/journey-scroll.ts): native scrolling everywhere, crossfade = pure function of scrollY, each stage = var(--journey-stage) of scroll (100svh touch / 75vh desktop), JOURNEY_HOLD 0.72 (fine-pointer desktop: DESKTOP_JOURNEY_HOLD 0.5, plus a ~180ms eased display follow of scrollY that never leads it) holds the stage, the rest crossfades (smootherstep). The only assist is settleTarget: a rest position inside a crossfade is finished to the nearer edge (≤ half a window). Never add wheel/touch preventDefault, CSS scroll-snap, glides, paging, or anything that discards or delays input.

- Traffic tag sides are chosen once per display window with a look-ahead along the airplane path (fitTagSide in traffic-ui.tsx); no per-frame re-layout, positions snap to whole pixels, and a callout with no clear side is skipped, because per-frame flipping made tags jitter.
- Call placement uses the pure traffic-call-placement helper with screen-pixel leader caps and projected runway-relative side preference; panel collisions flip sides rather than extending leaders, keeping zoom independent of placement.
- Card placement is WeakMap-locked until out of bounds; stable contain-fit media prevents jumps. Airplane selection is one click/tap on every device (no hover open, no tap-again-to-fleet): the card stays until a click outside it or Escape; only its "View in fleet"/"Track" button leaves the page (owner request).
- An open airplane info card registers in the shared traffic-card-open store (src/lib/traffic-card-open.ts): the home journeys pause auto-advance while it is up, and the tap that closes it is swallowed (flag read at window capture, which runs before the card's own document-capture close) so closing a card never also flips the stage.
- TrafficPath derives lines and final extension from patternWaypoints used by the flight engine; labels are placed only after mount in constant screen pixels with live UI/card collision checks, so maps, aircraft and labels share geometry without hydration-dependent rendering.
- Both the airport-map drawn pattern and the Private Pilot oblique pattern line pulse with the shared usePatternPulse hook (traffic-path.tsx, site_settings.traffic_pattern_mode off/pulse/always): shown ~3 s every 6–8 s on maps and ~2.5 s every 15–20 s on the oblique view (OBLIQUE_PATTERN_PULSE), 600 ms CSS fade, paused while a card is open, never in reduced motion (owner request, 2026-11-04).
- Radio calls hold CALL_DURATION_MS (traffic-flight.ts); the calling plane is marked with .is-calling (pulsing target, full leader) and the SVG gets .has-call to dim other history trails, all in CSS so timing and placement code stay untouched.
- Sequencing is shared by both views: equal-period slots reserve final/runway/departure plus 5s. Real-speed rollout/climb, small arrival extension and departure-side upwind/downwind elongation absorb delay without recurring circles; validate spacing/order and <0.2 circles/lap. KLXL aerial has Apache/Phoenix/Cardinal. Groups carry legOf data-leg.
- Every airplane always shows a target ring (15px minimum, grows with the airplane in the oblique view) with a dark under-stroke, and [data-aircraft] carries a unit-relative dark drop-shadow, so tiny planes read on bright fields without changing scale or hit areas.
- GLB sources live in models-src/; public/models is packed from them with `gltfpack -cc -vpf -vt 12 -vn 8` and decoded by the self-hosted public/models/meshopt_decoder.js registered in aircraft-model.tsx. Never re-pack an already packed file (it collapsed geometry once); scripts/check-models.mjs guards this in build. Material polish (paint clearcoat, tinted BLEND glass, metal props, matte tyres) is applied to models-src copies by name before packing; card models pack with textures capped at 2048px so they stay small enough to warm ahead of a tap.
- Live-ops (live-traffic.ts, /api/public/live-traffic via adsb.lol, 8s cache, routeset origin/destination cached 10 min): airport maps (traffic-pattern.tsx, mapPixel from the runway-midpoint reference) and the Private Pilot oblique view both switch to a live view (LIVE chip, dead-reckoned real aircraft, sim faded out) only when patternTraffic finds aircraft within 4 nm / 3,000 ft AGL (maps also require them inside the visible map); school tail numbers draw as their own airplane art. School tail numbers open their fleet card; others link to globe.adsb.lol by hex. ?livedemo (dev only) injects sample traffic.
- The oblique view draws N8570B/N4383P/N46201 from heading sprite atlases baked from models-src GLBs at the camera pitch (oblique-sprites.ts, 36 frames, frame = track minus line-of-sight bearing) at true literal scale (OBLIQUE_PLANE_BOOST = 1); other regs keep vectors. Target rings are sized per frame to 1.6x the on-screen span (ringRadius/setTagRing), min 11 px radius.
- Airport traffic always flies the pattern even in IFR (owner request); weather only shapes runway, wind, climb.
- Phone Story mode (site_settings.mobile_journey_mode, admin switch, read after mount so prerender stays scroll; dev ?story forces it) reuses FlightJourney: sections become one 100lvh screen, the viewport is touch-action pan-y: horizontal drags follow the finger and commit on flick-direction or past halfway (Instagram-style). Vertical movement between sections is native scrolling locked by CSS scroll snap (story-snap.ts: html.story-snapping mandatory, .story-snap anchors with snap-stop always, snapping off below the last anchor, a drag starting between the Locations anchor and the content anchor follows its direction, only upward free momentum relocks, a rest between anchors glides to the nearest); script never moves the page per finger frame or bounds momentum, because script-driven drags, glides and momentum caps made section slides change speed and stutter on iPhone. HUD shows the altitude climb; finger swipes turn the whole page as an Instagram cube (edge-hinged rotateY, perspective and will-change only while turning, all removed at rest so vertical slides stay flat; owner re-requested it), taps and auto-advance crossfade; forward taps or swipes on the last stage exit to the next section and backward on the first stage re-enters the previous story at its last stage (story-nav.ts; jumps pause snapping for two frames, and location shortcuts (hero chips, "Our locations") use the same instant jump, because smooth scrolls across snap anchors died on iPhone). Crossing sections turns one continuous cube: a static clone of the neighbour's facing page is the adjacent face, and the page jump happens only after it has fully turned in (no blank screen, no second turn). Double-tap never zooms in (pinch only). Phone stories auto-advance every 5s only until the visitor first touches that section (then they stay put), hold while a finger is down, loop from the last stage back to the first. Computers use a looping Carousel (site_settings.desktop_journey_mode, separate admin switch, dev ?carousel): 5s per stage, pause/prev/next pill, arrow keys, trackpad sideways swipe; vertical scrolling stays native.
- Journey sections carry data-offstage (set by an IntersectionObserver, no React render) when scrolled away or, in Story mode, covered by the next sibling; traffic rAF loops, videos, autoplay and CSS animations idle there, and Story layers get perspective/backface only while turning, because always-on work under covered sections made section slides stutter. Scroll-linked CSS variables are written on their own elements, never on <html>.
- Phone Story opening position has one owner (story-snap start): snapping stays off until fonts are ready and page height is still, then one instant top move arms it; never fight snap with repeating scroll timers, they caused load flashing.
- Home/logo returns use page-return.ts to suspend snap/settling and cancel queued section exits until the native glide stops. Menu returns start after the sheet unlocks scrolling, without restoring focus to its offscreen trigger.
- Map 3D airplanes (map-models-3d.tsx, lazy three.js, one canvas per active aerial map) never move anything: each frame they copy the SVG [data-aircraft] group's CTM (position, heading, wingspan px) and bank from heading rate, and mark the group data-model3d to hide its flat art only after the model draws, so animation, tags, cards and fallbacks stay owned by the SVG. Map GLBs in public/models/map are simplified copies packed from models-src with 512px paint; the oblique view keeps baked sprites, gated per plane by the same setting.
- Story cube faces stay painted: at rest the adjacent pages (and, on a first/last stage, the neighbour section's copied page) lie flat under the current one (data-warm), a face on screen at release finishes its turn before hiding, and a face's hinge never changes at rel 0, because hidden-until-turned pages and an instantly-hidden outgoing page flashed dark navy on quick swipes.
- The first-arrival swipe cue also turns the real page slightly toward the next one and back (storyPeek in story-nav.ts, painted through paintStory without transitions); any touch cancels it flat, and it is skipped when the next face would be empty or motion is reduced.
