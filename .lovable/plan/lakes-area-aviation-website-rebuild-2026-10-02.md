# Lakes Area Aviation Website Rebuild

## Goal
Rebuild the full Lakes Area Aviation website as a modern, mobile-first recruitment experience that preserves the school’s identity and information while converting more accelerated-training students.

The opening message will be direct and timeline-led:

**CFI and CFII in 4–6 Weeks.**  
**1 Hour Oral. 1 Hour Flight.**

CFI/CFII will be the strongest proof point, while the site will clearly promote accelerated Private Pilot, Instrument, Commercial Single, Commercial Multi, MEI, and Zero-to-Hero pathways as well.

## Visual direction
- Use the supplied Lakes Area Aviation aircraft logo prominently.
- Preserve the existing brand family: black, warm white, and rust orange around `#DD6D3F`.
- Retain the existing editorial typography character: Andada SC-style headings with Montserrat/Poppins supporting text.
- Open with a full-viewport, full-bleed aircraft photo or cinematic treatment with concise text directly over it.
- Use authentic school aircraft, instructor, location, and student imagery as primary evidence; generated imagery will only supplement gaps and will not imply a specific aircraft, person, facility, or outcome.
- Add smooth image drift, horizon parallax, runway-style scroll progress, staggered reveals, and animated training-path transitions, with reduced-motion support.
- Build the mobile experience deliberately: readable full-screen opening, compact navigation, touch-friendly controls, and persistent Call and Apply actions.

## Site structure
Create a complete multi-page site with shared navigation and footer, preserving the useful content and public pathways from the current site while consolidating duplicate or thin pages.

### Primary pages
1. **Home** — immersive opening, accelerated pathway overview, CFI/CFII timeline proof, testimonials, logistics, fleet, locations, and lead form.
2. **Accelerated Training** — overview of every accelerated rating and how an intensive stay works.
3. **CFI & CFII** — flagship conversion page centered on the 4–6 week timeline, 1-hour oral, 1-hour flight, prerequisites, preparation, daily rhythm, housing, transport, FAQs, and inquiry form.
4. **Zero-to-Hero** — complete career pathway with a visual progress sequence and all current cost information retained.
5. **Private Pilot**
6. **Instrument Rating**
7. **Commercial Single-Engine**
8. **Commercial Multi-Engine**
9. **MEI**
10. **Local Training**
11. **Specialized Training and Services**
12. **Introductory Flights** — retain the current $100 offer.
13. **Scenic Flights**
14. **Fleet** — every current aircraft and individual aircraft detail content, with broken or duplicate paths cleaned up.
15. **Rates** — all current wet, instruction, regular, and prepay rates presented clearly with applicable notes.
16. **Team** — instructors, credentials, and authentic photos.
17. **Student Success** — all existing testimonials, led by the accelerated CFI/CFII account.
18. **Locations** — Little Falls, Fort Lupton, and Cottonwood information, pending confirmation that all three remain active.
19. **About**
20. **New Students / Student Resources**
21. **Contact / Apply** — a qualified accelerated-training inquiry experience.

Existing public URLs will remain usable through matching pages or redirects where content is consolidated.

## Conversion journey
- Keep **Accelerated Training** and **Apply** visible in the main navigation.
- Use one dominant opening action: **Build My Training Plan**.
- Add contextual actions near pricing, aircraft, instructors, testimonials, and program requirements.
- Use the verified Andrew Cashen testimonial verbatim near the first major inquiry point.
- Surface logistics before they become objections: $30/night student housing and $20–30/day resident cars.
- Use a short first-step form, then collect program, current certificates, preferred location, target start date, travel needs, and notes.
- Add persistent mobile actions for calling the relevant location and applying.

## Content and factual safeguards
- Migrate the exact useful copy, prices, aircraft details, testimonials, contact information, photos, and any downloadable material found on the existing site.
- Do not invent pass rates, guarantees, completion times, instructor credentials, aircraft counts, cohort availability, or job-placement claims.
- Treat the user-provided **CFI and CFII in 4–6 Weeks / 1 Hour Oral / 1 Hour Flight** language as the approved flagship offer.
- Present the existing 10-day CFI and 12-day CFI-I testimonial as one named student’s experience, not a universal guarantee.
- Resolve the current “two locations” versus three listed locations inconsistency before presenting a definitive location count.
- Do not reproduce unsupported language generated in early visual concepts.

## Technical implementation
- Use separate search-friendly pages with unique titles and descriptions.
- Build reusable structures for program pages, aircraft pages, testimonials, rate tables, location details, forms, and mobile actions.
- Store the supplied logo and reused site media through the project’s managed asset delivery.
- Use semantic design tokens for the complete palette and typography system.
- Use modern image formats, stable media dimensions, lazy loading below the fold, and accessible contrast and form labels.
- Implement animation with performant transforms and opacity, never at the expense of reading or mobile stability.
- Keep inquiry handling presentation-ready; persistent lead delivery will require Lovable Cloud or an agreed external destination before launch.

## Validation
- Verify every navigation item, legacy path, form state, phone link, and program cross-link.
- Compare migrated rates, contact details, testimonials, fleet details, and program facts against the source site.
- Test desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, including the full-screen opening and persistent actions.
- Check reduced-motion behavior, keyboard navigation, contrast, overflow, image crops, and text legibility.
- Confirm all pages compile and render without runtime or network errors.
